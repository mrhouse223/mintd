Launch ArcSwap at a 2,000 start, then take 51%

The dev-buy cap is immutable at 5% of supply on the live pad, so 51% cannot be bought inside the launch transaction. It does not need to be: the launch position runs from the launch tick to MAX_TICK, so buying a fraction f of supply costs startMcap × f/(1-f) whether it happens in one transaction or two. This page launches with a dev buy just under the cap, then buys the rest on the open market until the wallet holds 51%.

There is a window between the launch and the follow-up buy. The token is public the moment it launches, so anyone watching can buy before your second transaction lands. That does not cost you the position, it just makes the remainder more expensive, because their buy moves the price up the same curve. Send step 3 immediately after step 2.
51% in one wallet is visible to everyone and reads as majority control. Anyone can see a single address holding a majority of supply, and it is the first thing a buyer checks. The liquidity is still permanently locked in the launchpad and you cannot withdraw it, which is the part that protects holders, but the concentration is real and worth stating publicly rather than being discovered.
